About me

Nick (STRAZDINE)

CREATIVE PROFESSIONAL

I’m a creative and technical professional with extensive experience spanning music production, audio and video editing, sound design, digital content, e-commerce, and website development. Over the years, my music production work has accumulated more than 500 million audio streams and views, and I’ve collaborated with over 1,000 artists, brands, and companies, including Warner Music, PUMA, Ethika, and Trailer Park Boys Nelk Boys, Shady Records, and more. Alongside my creative work, I’ve developed hands-on experience building digital projects, managing online stores, and working with technical systems and practical, detail-oriented tasks. I enjoy learning new tools, solving problems, and bringing ideas to life, whether that means producing a record, editing content, building a website, or improving a business workflow. I bring a versatile skill set, an independent work ethic, and a strong focus on quality, adaptability, and delivering results.

I’m always eager to take on new challenges, collaborate with great people, and apply my experience to projects where I can make a meaningful impact.
